Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Polaris Works ("we", "us") handles information in the apps we publish — web apps, progressive web apps (PWAs), desktop apps and Android apps ("the apps"). Where an app needs something different, its page or store listing and this policy will say so.

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. Unless an app's page or listing states otherwise, we do not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts or other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Settings and content you create in an app are stored locally on your device (for web apps and PWAs, in your browser's storage). Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes this data.

Permissions

Apps request only the permissions needed for their features, whether those are Android permissions or browser permissions. Each permission is used solely for the purpose described in the app.

Third-party services

If an app uses a third-party service (for example, crash reporting or Google Play services), that service may process limited technical data under its own privacy policy. Any such service is listed on the app's page or in its store listing, such as the Google Play data safety section.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.
