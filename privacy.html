Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains how Polaris Works ("we", "us") handles information in the Android apps we publish on Google Play ("the apps"). Where an app needs something different, its Google Play listing and this page will say so.

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. Unless an app's listing states otherwise, we do not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts or other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Settings and content you create in an app are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Permissions

Apps request only the Android permissions needed for their features. Each permission is used solely for the purpose described in the app.

Third-party services

If an app uses a third-party service (for example, crash reporting or Google Play services), that service may process limited technical data under its own privacy policy. Any such service is listed in the app's Google Play data safety section.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/polaris-works/polaris-works.github.io.
